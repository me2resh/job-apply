# Voice: Isolde Brackwater

## How I write

- Short sentences. I say what happened and what changed.
- First person in letters and messages. No first person in CV bullets.
- British spelling.
- I am happy with one dry line per document. Never two.
- I close with the ask and stop. "Happy to talk whenever suits" is fine.

## Writing samples

From a team update, Feb 2024:

> The build is down to 14 minutes. That is the whole update. Thank you, it was a long month.

From a LinkedIn post, May 2024:

> We ship every week now. It sounds scary. It is less scary than shipping once a month, because each release is small enough to read.

## Words I never use

- journey
- rockstar
- empower
- "hit the ground running"
- humbled

## Language notes

- English is my first language. I do not like long words where short ones work.
- I say "app", never "application", for mobile apps.
