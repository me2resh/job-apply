# Senior Engineering Manager, Mobile

Brindlecove. Remote (UK), with a team day in Sheffield once a month. GBP 95,000 - 110,000 base, plus share options.

Sample job ad: the company is invented.

## About us

Brindlecove is the booking app for community sport. Players use it to book pitches, courts and classes at about 1,200 venues across the UK, and around 400,000 people book through us every month.

## The role

You will lead our mobile team of 6 engineers (Android and iOS) and grow it to 10 over the next year. You report to the CTO and work day to day with the Head of Product and our design lead.

## What you will do

- Own the stability and speed of our apps. Our crash rate went up last year and we want it back down.
- Move us from releases every three weeks to something faster and safer.
- Run experiments with product on the booking funnel.
- Hire 4 engineers and help the team grow.
- Decide with the team whether we share code between Android and iOS.

## What we are looking for

Essential:

- Line management of a mobile team (Android and iOS) of 5 or more.
- A record of improving app stability and release practice, with numbers.
- Experience running A/B tests or other experiments with product.
- Hiring and growing engineers.
- Close work with product and design.

Desirable:

- Kotlin Multiplatform.
- Payments in a consumer app.
- Experience managing managers.
