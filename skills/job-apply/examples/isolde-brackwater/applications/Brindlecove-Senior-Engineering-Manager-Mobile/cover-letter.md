Dear Hiring Team,

I lead a team of 7 at Quillmarsh, including 5 Android and iOS engineers. Quillmarsh is a marketplace where people hire tools and equipment from local depots. For four years my job has been to make a consumer booking app stable, then fast to change. I would like to do that at Brindlecove.

In Q3 2022 the apps released once a month and crash-free sessions sat at 97.1%. Each release was large, so each one was risky, so we released less. We put a health gate on every staged rollout: nothing goes wider below 99.3% crash-free. I ran crash triage every week, the engineers fixed what it found, and we moved to a weekly release train with a written checklist. By Q2 2023 crash-free sessions were at 99.6%. In 2023 we shipped 46 releases, against 11 the year before.

Small, safe releases made experiments cheap. I led the engineering side of a 6-week A/B test of a new checkout, with our product manager and designer. Booking conversion went from 18% to 23%, and we shipped it to everyone in November 2023.

I also grew the team from 3 to 7, with 4 hires in 2023, and promoted two engineers. I wrote the interview loop and ran every final interview.

On sharing code between Android and iOS: I built a Kotlin Multiplatform proof of concept in 2024 and we chose to stay native. I would happily walk you through how we made that call.

I am available one month after an offer. Happy to talk whenever suits.
