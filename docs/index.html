Tailored job applications that are true in every line.

Job Apply is an open-source add-on for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and GitHub Copilot. It keeps your career facts in one bank, writes each CV and cover letter from that bank only, and has a separate reviewer attack them before you send anything.

/plugin install job-apply --marketplace me2resh/job-apply
Codex, Cursor, Copilot and others

From the example's real review file. The person, the company and every number are invented.

Facts first. Writing second.

Most tailored CVs fail in one of two ways: they are generic, or they stretch the truth to fit the ad. Job Apply fixes both by keeping the facts apart from the writing.

The first time you run it, it builds your fact bank by interview. It reads your CV, LinkedIn export and reviews, then asks for what they leave out: the number, the baseline, the period, your part against the team's, and the evidence. It accepts "no number" and writes that down too.

After that, give it a job ad. It checks the role against your own filters, maps each criterion in the ad to a fact, and writes a CV and cover letter in your voice. The documents can be shaped to the ad. They can never say more than the bank holds.

Everything lives in one folder you choose, as plain markdown and YAML you can read and edit. Nothing leaves your machine except the job ad fetches.

How an application runs

Six steps, in this order, every time. Each one leaves a file you can read. Here is what each step left for the example.

  1. 1

    Fetch the ad

    The page first. When the careers page blocks scripts, the hiring system's public JSON, then an archived copy. If all of those fail, it asks you to paste the ad.

    jd.mdfront matter
    fetched:     2026-10-28
fetch_route: careers page returned 403; read from the ATS public JSON
  2. 2

    Gate

    Your own filters, set at setup: pay floor, location and office days, right to work, industries and employers you will not work for, seniority, must-haves. Plus a scale check of their numbers against your largest true ones. You see the verdict before a word is written.

    applications.mdthree roles, three verdicts
    Brindlecove, Senior Engineering Manager, MobileEvery gate passes. Not a stretch role on scale.
    APPLY
    Glintmoor Metrics, Head of MobileCore product is in-app ad attribution: an industry she excluded.
    SKIP
    Unnamed company, via a recruiterCompany unnamed, so the industry gate cannot run. Band not stated.
    QUALIFY FIRST
  3. 3

    Map

    Each criterion in the ad against your fact ids, marked strong, partial or gap. Gaps are told to you plainly. They are never papered over in the documents.

    gate-and-mapping.mdprivate, never sent
    Line management of a mobile team of 5 or more7 direct reports: 5 mobile engineers, 1 backend, 1 QA
    strong
    Close work with product and designEvidence is one project; say it plainly
    partial
    Kotlin MultiplatformSay "built a proof of concept", never "experience in production"
    partial
    Managing managersNone. The documents stay silent; prepare an answer.
    gap
  4. 4

    Write

    A CV to a fixed, ATS-safe skeleton and a 250 to 350 word letter, in your voice, using the ad's own words where they are honestly true.

    cv.mdthe skeleton
    ## Profile
## Key Achievements
## Professional Experience
## Earlier Career
## Talks and Open Source
## Education
  5. 5

    Check and review

    A script fails the documents on dashes, banned phrases, star counts and untraced numbers. Then a separate reviewer, a cynical talent partner, returns SHIP or FIX with line-level findings and the questions an interviewer would ask. Up to two rounds.

    review.mdthe example's rounds
    Round 1, CV6 findings, all applied
    FIX
    Round 1, cover letter5 findings, all applied
    FIX
    Round 2, CV and letterOne optional polish on the CV, applied
    SHIP
  6. 6

    Render

    PDFs with generic filenames, one folder per application, a tracker row, and a reminder. It never sends anything. You do, after the human pass.

    applications/Brindlecove-Senior-Engineering-Manager-Mobile/
    Isolde-Brackwater-CV.pdf
Isolde-Brackwater-Cover-Letter.pdf

Read it aloud once. Rewrite at least one sentence in your own words.

The rules it will not break

When two rules pull apart, the earlier one wins. The ad comes last: a criterion in the ad never licenses breaking a house rule.

  1. Honesty
  2. Anti-cringe
  3. Voice
  4. Reviewer findings
  5. The ad
  • Every claim traces to your fact bank.A missing fact is asked for or left out. New facts go into the bank first, then into documents.
  • Durations round down. Headcounts are exact.A single report is never made plural. Influence is never written as line management.
  • A recent skill is not stretched by the length of your career.A proof of concept is never written as production experience.
  • The gate runs before any writing.Only on filters you set. A SKIP stops the run unless you override it, and the override is logged.
  • No dashes, no AI-tell phrases, no flattery.No em or en dashes, no praise for the company, no telling readers what they already know.
  • No hobbies, photos, self-ratings or star counts on a CV.Even when the ad asks for "passion". That evidence goes to the letter or the interview.
  • The author never reviews its own work.The reviewer runs as a separate agent or a fresh context, with its own brief.
  • It never sends anything.You read the final text aloud and rewrite at least one sentence in your own words.
FIX

Round 1 said FIX

The reviewer works from the ad, the fact bank and the voice file, and nothing the writer thought. On the example's first drafts it caught two real inflation errors. Here are its findings, lightly trimmed, and what changed.

READ TEST: A mobile engineering manager whose page 1 covers the JD's top three criteria in its own words, but the first line of the Profile says the team is "7 Android and iOS engineers", and the scope line under it says only 5 are mobile engineers.

  1. 1 · InflationApplied
    -Draft: with four years leading a team of 7 Android and iOS engineers
    +Fix: A team of 7 reports to me, including 5 Android and iOS engineers.

    WHYThe fact bank says 5 mobile engineers, 1 backend engineer and 1 QA engineer, and she started with 3 in Apr 2022. So this is wrong twice: the team was not 7 for four years, and it is not 7 mobile engineers. The scope line on the same page contradicts it, and the JD's first essential criterion is exactly this.

  2. 2 · InflationApplied
    -Draft: which made weekly releases cheap
    +Fix: Sponsored the Android CI work that cut build time from 38 to 14 minutes, Feb 2024.

    WHYWeekly releases started in Jan 2023 and the build cut was Feb 2024, so the build work cannot have made weekly releases possible. A hiring manager who checks the dates catches a made-up cause.

  3. 3 · AI tells and voiceApplied
    -Draft: The team chose to keep two native apps, and I can explain why.
    +Fix: Built a Kotlin Multiplatform proof of concept for shared pricing logic, Jun 2024. Not shipped: the team chose to keep two native apps.

    WHYThe voice file says no first person in CV bullets, and "I can explain why" teases the reader instead of stating a fact.

  4. 4 · InflationApplied after a fact bank update
    Full right to work in the UK, no sponsorship needed

    WHYThis is a factual claim about her status, and the fact bank does not hold it. If it is wrong, it ends the process.

    THENIsolde confirmed it, it went into the fact bank first with a dated change log line, and only then stayed on the CV.

  5. 5 · Length and structureApplied
    -Cut from Key Achievements: Handled 9,000 ticket orders in the first hour of a major on-sale with no downtime, after leading the load testing and adding a virtual queue.

    WHYA 2021 result from her tech lead role sits on page 1 with no company or role attached, so it reads as current app work. It answers none of the JD's criteria. Cut from Key Achievements; the Tidewren section carries it.

  6. 6 · InflationApplied
    -Draft: At Quillmarsh, a bookings marketplace
    +Fix: At Quillmarsh, a marketplace for hiring tools and garden equipment,

    WHYThe fact bank describes Quillmarsh as a marketplace for hiring tools and garden equipment. "Bookings marketplace" looks reshaped to match the hiring company's own pitch.

READ TEST: A manager who fixed app stability and release cadence with real numbers, then used that to run a checkout experiment and grow the team, but the opening line overstates how many of her 7 are Android and iOS engineers.

  1. 1 · InflationApplied
    -Draft: I lead a team of 7 Android and iOS engineers at Quillmarsh
    +Fix: I lead a team of 7 at Quillmarsh, including 5 Android and iOS engineers.

    WHYTwo of the 7 are a backend engineer and a QA engineer. The letter's first sentence contradicted the CV on the JD's first essential criterion.

  2. 2 · InflationApplied
    -Draft: With our product manager and designer I ran a 6-week A/B test of a new checkout.
    +Fix: I led the engineering side of a 6-week A/B test of a new checkout, with our product manager and designer.

    WHYThe fact bank gives her the engineering side and the experiment flags. The product manager owned the hypothesis and the designer designed the flow. "I ran" claims the whole test.

  3. 3 · Wrong tone for the readerApplied
    -Draft: because the hiring bar is the manager's job
    +Fix: I wrote the interview loop and ran every final interview.

    WHYIt explains the basics of management to a CTO.

  4. 4 · Neediness and AI tellsApplied
    -Draft: I would happily walk you through that call, because it was not the obvious one.
    +Fix: I would happily walk you through how we made that call.

    WHYA teaser that hints at a story instead of giving the one fact that would make the reader want it.

  5. 5 · Reader-blindnessApplied
    -Draft: I would like to do that for the Senior Engineering Manager, Mobile role.
    +Fix: I would like to do that at Brindlecove.

    WHYIt tells them their own job title back, and the company is never named anywhere in the letter.

READ TEST, CV: An engineering manager with four years in the role and a team of 7, including 5 Android and iOS engineers. Page 1 answers the JD's top three criteria in its own words and with numbers.

  1. 1 · Wrong tone for the reader · optional polishApplied, shorter
    -Draft: Engineering manager for consumer mobile apps, with four years as an engineering manager
    +Fix: Engineering manager for consumer mobile apps: four years in the role and 14 years building software.

    WHYThe first sentence says "engineering manager" twice, so it stumbles when read aloud.

Cover letter: SHIP Findings: none.

What an interviewer would ask

Every round also returns the questions the documents invite. That list is the interview prep. A few from round 1:

  • What the team actually looks like, and when it reached 7.
  • How much of the 97.1% to 99.6% gain came from the gate and the triage, and how much from the engineers' fixes.
  • Why the Kotlin Multiplatform proof of concept did not ship.
  • Taking the team from 6 to 10 with no experience managing managers.

Install

Then ask: "Set up job apply". Later: "Apply for this: https://..." or "Reply to this recruiter: ...".

One line, inside Claude Code:

/plugin install job-apply --marketplace me2resh/job-apply

Older Claude Code, two steps:

/plugin marketplace add me2resh/job-apply /plugin install job-apply@job-apply

From the shell:

claude plugin install job-apply --marketplace me2resh/job-apply

The skill answers to /job-apply, and the plugin adds the job-apply-reviewer agent.

Clone the repo, then copy the skill into ~/.codex/skills/:

git clone https://github.com/me2resh/job-apply cd job-apply && ./install.sh codex

Without the plugin agent, the reviewer runs as a fresh context with references/reviewer.md as its brief.

Copy the rule file into your project:

mkdir -p .cursor/rules && cp job-apply/.cursor/rules/job-apply.mdc .cursor/rules/

Run it from the folder that holds your clone of the repo.

Copy .github/copilot-instructions.md into your project, or append its text to the instructions you already have.

mkdir -p .github && cat job-apply/.github/copilot-instructions.md >> .github/copilot-instructions.md

Any agent that reads the Agent Skills format can use the same folder. Copy it into that agent's skills directory:

./install.sh /path/to/your/agent/skills

Anything else: add the AGENTS.md text to its instructions and keep skills/job-apply/ where it can read it.

Needs

Python 3, standard library only. Chrome, Chromium, Edge or Brave for the PDFs; set CHROME=/path to pick one.

Optional

pdftotext from poppler, or pip install pypdf, turns on the PDF page-end and text-layer checks.

Your data

One folder you choose at setup, suggested ~/job-apply/. Nothing leaves your machine except the job ad fetches.
